# Claritty Code of Conduct

Claritty is open to people of all backgrounds and levels of experience. This code applies to participation in Claritty issues, pull requests, discussions, and other spaces the project officially uses.

## How we work together

- Treat people with respect. Give feedback on the code, design, or evidence, and explain disagreements in terms others can act on.
- Welcome questions and make room for new contributors. Share enough context for others to understand decisions and reproduce problems.
- Respect privacy. Get permission before sharing someone’s personal information, private conversations, or identifiable cluster data.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, unwanted sexual attention, and publishing someone’s private information without permission are grounds for moderation.

## Report a concern

Email the [Claritty maintainer](mailto:vaishnavsk8804@gmail.com?subject=Claritty%20conduct%20report) privately. Include what happened, where it happened, and any links or screenshots you are comfortable sharing. The maintainer will limit disclosure of the report to the people needed to address it.

Depending on the severity and history of the behavior, maintainers may remove content, issue a warning, or restrict participation temporarily or permanently.
